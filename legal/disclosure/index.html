Advertising disclosure

Display advertising on the resource pages. Nothing on the studio pages. No paid placements anywhere.

Last updated August 29, 2026

How this site makes money

JunePoint Resources is funded by display advertising served through Google AdSense. Advertisements are selected and delivered programmatically by Google based on page context and, where you have permitted it, your ad settings. We do not choose individual advertisers and generally do not know which ones appear on a given page.

Where ads appear and where they do not

AreaAdvertising
/tools/, /guides/, /reviews/, /resources/Yes. Display units appear within and alongside the content
The JunePoint Studio portfolio at /No. No ad units or advertising code are loaded
Legal, about and contact pagesNo

Ad units are labelled "Advertisement" and kept out of the navigation. They are not styled as editorial links or buttons. Please report any ad that appears to be part of the editorial content.

What advertising does not buy

  • Advertisers cannot pay for coverage or influence what is published.
  • Positions in buying guides are not for sale.
  • The site does not publish sponsored links, paid guest posts or link insertions.
  • Vendors do not receive pages for pre-publication review.

Affiliate relationships

We currently have none, and no page on this site contains an affiliate link. Should that change, every affected page will carry a disclosure above the content and this page will be updated first.

Free products and vendor relationships

JunePoint has not accepted free licences, hardware, paid travel or gifts in exchange for coverage. When the studio has commercial experience with a product, that relationship is stated on the page.

Controlling the ads you see

Personalised advertising can be turned off at Google Ads Settings, and third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
