Free Online Developer & Finance Tools

Calculators, formatters and converters that run entirely in your browser.

  • SaaS Seat Cost Calculator
  • Cloud Cost Calculator
  • Cron Expression Generator
  • Mortgage Payoff Calculator
  • Colour Contrast Checker
  • JWT Decoder
  • JSON Formatter and Validator
  • Base64 Encoder and Decoder

The tools run entirely in your browser. Inputs are not uploaded, logged or stored because there is no backend to receive them.

They are free, require no account and work offline after the page loads. Calculator pages state the assumptions that affect their results.
